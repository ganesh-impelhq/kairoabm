Stages

Four stages, and the honesty to include the first one

No reach

Not served

On the list, never delivered an impression. Usually an audience-size or budget-allocation problem, and always the cheapest thing to fix.

Aware

Reached, thin

Impressions delivered, one function, low frequency. Not a signal. Do not hand this to a rep.

Engaged

Multiple functions

Two or more job functions engaging inside the window, with repeat exposure. Worth a nurture sequence, not yet a call.

In market

Committee moving

Breadth, depth and seniority all present, with recent bottom-funnel engagement. This is the list your AE should get on Monday.

On the word “intent”: engagement with your own ads is first-party behavioural evidence, and it is genuinely predictive. It is not the same thing as third-party intent data, and Kairo will not describe it as such. If an account is in market and you did not detect it, Kairo says the sample was too small rather than inventing a score.
Signals out

A score is only useful where the work happens

Into the CRM

Score, stage, last movement and the top three contributing touches written onto the account record in HubSpot, Salesforce or Attio.

Into sequencing

Threshold crossings pushed to Clay, Outreach or Salesloft so an account entering In market can trigger a sequence without a human noticing first.

Into anything else

Signed webhooks on every transition, plus the REST API and MCP server if you would rather pull than be pushed.
