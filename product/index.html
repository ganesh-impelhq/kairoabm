Under it all

How the account graph is assembled

Four inputs, one resolved object. Nothing else in the product works until this does.

Your target list

Uploaded as CSV or pulled from the CRM. Domains are normalised, subsidiaries are grouped to the parent where you want them grouped, and duplicates are collapsed.

Ad delivery

Impressions, clicks and video views pulled from the ad platforms on a rolling schedule, then resolved to companies and job functions.

Firmographics

Industry, size band, region and technology signals, used both for matching and for the segment views you slice reporting by.

CRM objects

Accounts, opportunities, close dates and amounts, read back so attribution has something real to point at.

What Kairo does not do: it does not de-anonymise individual website visitors, and it does not sell you contact records. Person-level detail is limited to what the ad platforms report about engagement with your own campaigns.
