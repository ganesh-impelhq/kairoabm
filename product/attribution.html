Product / Revenue attribution

Influenced pipeline is the easiest number in B2B to inflate.

Set the touch definition too loosely and every opportunity you ever opened was influenced. Kairo makes the definition explicit, shows you what the number becomes under three stricter ones, and puts them side by side.

Account-matched, not session-matched

An opportunity is joined to the account, not to a cookie that survived a browser and a two-month sales cycle. This is the only join that holds up over a B2B buying window.

Cost per account moved

Pipeline arrives late and lumpy. Cost per account moved into Engaged, and into In market, is available weekly — which makes it the metric you can actually steer a campaign on.

Multi-channel timeline

LinkedIn, Google, Reddit and organic in one account chronology, so a self-reported source on the form finally has something to be checked against.

What you take into the QBR

The programme view

Coverage, movement and cost for one named-account programme. Answers “is this working” without requiring the reader to know what a CPM is.

The creative view

The segment view

Everything above, cut by tier, industry, region or size band — the cut that usually explains an average that looked fine.
