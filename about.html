About

We built the report we kept rebuilding by hand.

Kairo started as a recurring Monday morning job: export the LinkedIn delivery data, join it to a target list, work out which accounts had actually been reached, and try to explain to a sales team why the number in the deck did not match what they saw in the CRM.

Start a 30-day trialBook a walkthrough

Doing that by hand teaches you two things quickly. The first is that the join is not hard — it is just tedious, and tedium is what software is for. The second is more uncomfortable: almost every ABM report in circulation is measuring people and describing accounts, and the gap between those two things is where most programmes quietly fail.

So the product has one opinion, and it runs through every screen: start from the list you chose, not from the engagement you happened to find. That is why unreached accounts get a row of their own, why coverage comes before quality, and why every score expands into the touches that produced it. A measurement tool that only shows you your successes is a marketing tool for its own vendor.

What we will not build

  • Autopilot spending. Kai proposes; a person approves. An agent that reallocates a quarter's budget without a human in the loop is a liability dressed as a feature.
  • Contact databases. We do not sell you people's details, and we do not de-anonymise individual website visitors. Person-level data is limited to engagement with your own campaigns.
  • Percentage-of-spend pricing. A measurement vendor paid on your ad spend has an obvious reason to keep telling you to spend more.
  • Benchmarks that flatter us. Aggregate benchmarks are opt-in, computed on anonymised minimum cohorts, and published with the sample size attached.

Who builds it

Kairo is built by Abron Technologies Private Limited, a small product-led team based in Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala. There is no SDR function, no outbound sequence waiting for you at the end of this page, and no gate on the trial. If you want to talk to a person you can, and if you would rather just connect an ad account and look at your own data, that works too.
