Questions

Pricing questions we get asked

What counts as a target account?
A unique company on a target list in your workspace, whether or not it has ever been reached. We count unreached accounts deliberately — they are the ones you most need to see, and pricing that hid them would create the wrong incentive.
What happens if I go over the account limit?
Nothing breaks. Kairo keeps ingesting and reporting, and we email you with the overage and the options. We do not silently truncate a list or stop scoring mid-quarter.
Do you charge on ad spend?
No. A percentage-of-spend model gives a measurement vendor an obvious incentive to tell you to spend more, and we would rather not have that conversation with ourselves.
Can an agency put a client on their own plan?
Yes — that is what Agency is for. Each client gets an isolated workspace with its own lists, integrations and reporting, and you get a cross-client view the clients cannot see.
Is there a discount for early-stage companies?
Yes. Companies under 20 people and under $2m ARR get 50% off Starter or Growth for the first year. Email us from a company address and we will apply it.
What is actually in the trial?
Everything in Growth, for 30 days, with no card. That includes the backfill, so you will know within the first session whether the coverage picture is useful to you.
